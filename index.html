Coaching in Action
Not a knowledge problem.
A behavior problem.

A story worth telling
Everyone called it a skill problem.
It wasn't.

Technicians were avoiding certain repair types. The obvious diagnosis was skill gap. The obvious solution was training. I asked a different question: why are they avoiding it?

It was a confidence gap with a specific failure mode. A lecture wouldn't touch that. So I built a game instead: bingo cards of repair types, played in teams, with a rotating facilitator baked into the design. The game surfaced exactly who was avoiding what, without anyone having to say it out loud. Once the gap was visible, we could coach the actual thing. Behavior changed. No one sat through a single lecture.

I trained the facilitator. Then I walked away.

Still running.
